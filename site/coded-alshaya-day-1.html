AI Quickstart & the Art of Prompting

Start from zero: what an AI model is actually doing when it writes, where it saves you real time today, and the four-part framework that turns a vague request into a draft you can send.

Tuesday 1 September 2026 09:00 – 13:00 CODED Campus, Kuwait

Day brief

Most people's first experience with AI is disappointing, and the reason is almost always the prompt. Today we fix that. You'll see plainly what a large language model is and is not, run your first prompts in the room, then learn CTFT — Context, Task, Format, Tone — and use it to draft a real professional document, edit it into shape, and pull a summary out of a long report. Every exercise is on public, non-confidential material, so everything you practise here you can use at your desk tomorrow.

Run of show

A guide, not a script — timings flex to the room.

  1. 1
    Session 1 — What AI is, and why it matters at work09:00
    Software that reads, writes and reasons — what an LLM actually does under the hood, and where writing, research and problem-solving get faster today.
  2. 2
    Session 2 — Your first prompts09:40
    Everyone writes prompts live, then we compare them. What separates a prompt that works from one that doesn't: specificity, a clear purpose, real detail, and a stated tone. Lab L1.1 — first contact.
  3. Break10:20
  4. 3
    Session 3 — The CTFT framework10:35
    Context, Task, Format, Tone. Each element on its own, then all four together — with side-by-side poor and excellent prompts. Lab L1.2 — build a CTFT prompt on a stand-in document.
  5. 4
    Session 4 — Writing & communication with AI11:30
    The three moves — draft, rewrite, summarize — then email end to end, and what changes when you connect Claude straight to your inbox. Labs L1.3 & L1.4 — draft a document, then rewrite it without starting over.
  6. Break12:10
  7. 5
    Session 5 — Review, research & summaries12:20
    Editing AI output for accuracy, tone and structure — what to always verify. Labs L1.5 & L1.6 — tame an email thread, then summarise the quarterly report and catch its planted faults. Debrief and close.

What you'll leave able to do

  • Explain in plain terms what an LLM is and where it helps.
  • Write a prompt using Context, Task, Format and Tone.
  • Draft a professional document and iterate it to a sendable version.
  • Summarise a long document and know which parts to verify.
